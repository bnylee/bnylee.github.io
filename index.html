“A Reproducible Pipeline for Multi-Country Social Accounting Matrix Construction”

Mentor: Prof. Mikhail Oet · Business and Social Sciences

A Social Accounting Matrix is one big table of every payment in a country’s economy over a year. It shows who sells to whom, who earns wages and profit, and what the government collects, and every row has to add up to the same total as its column. My Python pipeline builds these tables from global data, balances them, and checks them. They’re built to calibrate a planned research model that will simulate how sanctions and supply shocks spread.

Reaction-Time Game

A Chernobyl-themed carnival game where you hit each input as it lights up, faster and faster, until the reactor melts down. I wrote the game logic in Python. Using stats from 30+ user trials, I tuned the speed so an average game ran under 2 minutes and calculated prize cutoffs so the game would turn a profit. I also designed and painted the outside.

About

I'm Benny Lee, a second-year Mechanical Engineering student at Northeastern University. I graduate with my BSME in May 2029.

Most of my projects live where a machine and its code have to work together, like a box with servo-driven doors or a game that times button presses to under a millisecond. That’s why I study both sides. Outside of class I’m an undergraduate researcher and PEAK Summit Fellow in Northeastern’s Financial Stress Lab, and every week I teach hands-on STEM workshops to K–8 students with STEMout.

Away from engineering, I’m a two-time national Taekwondo sparring champion and a three-time New York All-State track athlete. I speak Korean, play piano and guitar, and bring a camera whenever I travel or go snowboarding.

I'm looking for a spring 2027 co-op in mechanical engineering or computer science.
